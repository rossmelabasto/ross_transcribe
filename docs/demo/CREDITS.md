# Demo audio

Both clips are in the **public domain**.

| File | Source |
|---|---|
| `armstrong.ogg` | Neil Armstrong, Apollo 11, 20 July 1969 — "That's one small step for man…". NASA recording. [Wikimedia Commons: Armstrong Small Step.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Armstrong_Small_Step.ogg) |
| `quijote.ogg` | Miguel de Cervantes, *Don Quijote de la Mancha*, part I, chapter 1 (opening 31 seconds). LibriVox recording. [Wikimedia Commons: Quijote vol1 03 cervantes.mp3](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Quijote_vol1_03_cervantes.mp3), trimmed and re-encoded to Opus. |

The `.txt` and `.srt` files next to them are the actual output of `transcribe` (model `small`, auto-detected language, timestamps on). The GIFs in `docs/` were recorded with [asciinema](https://asciinema.org) and rendered with [agg](https://github.com/asciinema/agg).
